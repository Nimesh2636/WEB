6 Reasons Why Mulberry Silk Soap Is One of the Best Natural Soaps for Your Skin

By Nepal Silk & Pashmina Craft  ·  June 2025  ·  5 min read

Mulberry silk has been used in skincare for thousands of years. But does mulberry silk soap actually work in soap form? Here is what the research — and your skin — will tell you.

Mulberry silk soap is not a gimmick. Made from the finest silk produced by Bombyx mori silkworms that feed exclusively on mulberry leaves, this natural soap contains two powerful proteins — sericin and fibroin — that carry properties that genuinely benefit skin when absorbed during washing. Unlike many trendy skincare claims, the science behind mulberry silk soap is well-established. Below are six real reasons why mulberry silk soap is one of the best natural soaps available, explained plainly.

Reason 01Mulberry silk soap moisturises deeply without clogging pores

Sericin, the outer protein of the mulberry silk cocoon, is a natural humectant — meaning it draws moisture from the air and holds it against your skin. When present in natural mulberry silk soap, it leaves a fine conditioning layer after rinsing that keeps skin feeling soft without the greasy residue of oil-heavy products.

This makes mulberry silk protein soap particularly useful in summer: it hydrates without heaviness, and works equally well on dry, oily, and combination skin types.

Reason 02Mulberry silk soap supports collagen and slows visible ageing

Multiple peer-reviewed studies have found that sericin from mulberry silk stimulates collagen Type I synthesis in the skin — the kind responsible for firmness and elasticity. Regular use of natural mulberry silk soap has been associated with measurable improvements in skin elasticity and a reduction in fine lines over time.

What the research says: A published study concluded that sericin used in topical formulations produces increased skin elasticity and measurable anti-ageing effects. It also found sericin can suppress oxidative stress — a key driver of premature skin ageing.

Reason 03Mulberry silk soap delivers 18 amino acids your skin can absorb

Silk proteins from premium mulberry silk contain 18 essential amino acids — the same building blocks found in healthy human skin. Because the molecular structure of mulberry silk is close to that of skin tissue, these amino acids are absorbed rather than simply sitting on the surface. Handmade mulberry silk soap nourishes skin cells directly, contributing to a smoother texture and improved barrier function with regular use.

Reason 04Mulberry silk soap protects against UV damage and oxidative stress

Research has shown that mulberry silk sericin helps protect skin cells from UV-induced oxidative damage. It does this partly by suppressing cell apoptosis (premature cell death triggered by sun exposure) and partly through its antioxidant properties. While natural mulberry silk soap is not a substitute for sunscreen, daily use adds a genuine layer of cellular protection — especially relevant during the summer months.

Reason 05Mulberry silk soap produces a richer lather that is gentle on sensitive skin

Mulberry silk fibres improve both the texture and quality of soap lather — creating a creamier, smoother foam that glides across skin rather than dragging. This reduces micro-friction during cleansing, which matters for people with sensitive, reactive, or acne-prone skin. The lather of natural mulberry silk soap also rinses cleanly, leaving no residue.

Importantly, mulberry silk protein soap does not cause irritation and is safe for all skin types, including children's skin — a fact supported by clinical soap safety testing.

Reason 06Mulberry silk soap gradually reduces uneven skin tone

Mulberry silk sericin has demonstrated mild inhibitory effects on melanin production, which contributes to a gradual evening of skin tone and reduction in dullness. This is not a bleaching effect — it is a gentle, natural process that supports the skin's own renewal cycle. With consistent use of natural mulberry silk soap, many people notice their complexion looks clearer and more uniform.

  • Locks in moisture without grease — ideal for daily summer use
  • Stimulates collagen production for firmer, smoother skin over time
  • Delivers 18 amino acids that skin can actually absorb
  • Provides antioxidant and mild UV-protective support
  • Creates a gentle, rich lather — safe for all skin types
  • Gradually evens skin tone through natural sericin activity
