About Me

Creativity
Meets Strategy

I am Shrijan — a digital marketing professional with a passion for growing brands online through data-driven strategies and compelling storytelling. My work spans SEO, social media, content creation, and paid campaigns.

Beyond the screen, I carry the music of Nepal in my hands — from the resonant dhime drum to the soulful flute. I teach and perform, preserving tradition while embracing modern artistry.

I am currently pursuing a Master's degree in Psychology, deepening my understanding of human behaviour to craft marketing and communication that truly connects.
